Installation

Get running on Windows in 3 steps.

Once your download finishes, follow these steps to open the application.

1

Extract the archive

Unzip the downloaded archive and extract the .vhdx container file to a folder on your PC.

2

Mount the VHDX

Double-click the .vhdx file to mount it as a virtual drive, then open the drive and enter its folder.

3

Launch the app

Double-click the executable inside the folder to open Kestrel and start exploring ads.

After launching: Click Start, choose the trial plan, select a server location, and enable the remaining options to begin browsing.
FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to log in with my Facebook account?

No. You don't need to link your personal profile or ad account to search and inspect ads.

Which versions of Windows are supported?

Kestrel is built for 64-bit versions of Windows 10 and Windows 11.

Can I save ads for later reference?

Yes. You can bookmark campaigns and export creatives locally so your team can review hooks and angles anytime.

Why isn't the VHDX mounting automatically?

Make sure the archive is fully extracted first—Windows cannot mount a virtual disk directly from inside a compressed ZIP.
