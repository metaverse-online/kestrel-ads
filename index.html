Installation

Get running on Windows in 3 steps.

Once your download finishes, follow these steps to open the software.

1

Extract the archive

Unzip the downloaded archive and extract the .vhdx container file to a folder on your PC.

2

Mount the VHDX

Double-click the .vhdx file to mount it as a virtual drive, then open the drive and enter its folder.

3

Launch the software

Double-click the executable inside the folder to open Kestrel and start researching winning products.

After launching: Click Start, choose the trial plan, select a server location, and enable the remaining options to begin browsing.
FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to connect any personal social or store accounts?

No. Kestrel works independently on your desktop—you never need to link personal accounts or ad managers.

Which versions of Windows are supported?

The software is built for 64-bit versions of Windows 10 and Windows 11.

Can I export ad videos and product links locally?

Yes. You can download video creatives and save storefront URLs directly to your computer for easy team sharing.

Why isn't the VHDX mounting automatically?

Make sure the archive is fully extracted first—Windows cannot mount a virtual disk directly from inside a compressed ZIP.
